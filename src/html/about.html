About HelvetiBase

HelvetiBase was born in the heart of Switzerland, where precision engineering meets breathtaking landscapes. Our founders, a small team of database engineers from Zürich and Lausanne, shared a simple conviction: deploying databases should be as effortless, reliable, and elegant as a Swiss railway timetable.

We set out to remove the friction from database deployment—no more brittle scripts, snowflake servers, or nights lost to manual failovers. With HelvetiBase, you define intent, and we handle the rest: provisioning, clustering, high availability, backups, security hardening, and observability—all automated, audit-ready, and reproducible.

Built on a foundation of strong cryptography and cloud-agnostic architecture, our platform lets teams spin up production-grade PostgreSQL, MySQL, and distributed data stores in minutes, across regions and clouds, with consistent guardrails. Scale elastically when traffic surges, roll back safely when needed, and ship with confidence—without becoming an infrastructure expert.

Switzerland taught us to value trust, resilience, and craftsmanship. We bring those values to every deployment pipeline we design. Whether you are a startup launching your first product or an enterprise modernizing legacy systems, HelvetiBase makes database deployment predictable, secure, and delightfully simple.
